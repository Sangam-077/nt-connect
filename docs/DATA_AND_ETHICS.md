# Data sources, provenance and responsible use

This is an educational, exploratory project built from source-derived datasets. Keep the raw copies and cleaning notebooks in the existing repository or in a separate reproducibility folder subject to original source terms.

| Layer | Source / scope | Important caveat |
|---|---|---|
| NT Remote Areas Mobile Coverage | NT Government mobile-location spreadsheet | Infrastructure flags, not measured signal maps or performance |
| Mobile Black Spot projects | Government programme point locations/status | Reporting period and project status may differ from mobile source; no assumed cross-file duplicate equivalence |
| ASGS 2021 remoteness | ABS geographic polygons | Remote/Very Remote classification is spatial context |
| Population | Mobile source when recorded | Missing is unknown, not zero; not a live census |
| NBN 2024 | Supplied **fixed-line** polygon | Does not assess satellite or fixed wireless |
| Historical tropical cyclones | BOM best-track database | Historical proximity, not future hazard, return period or damage likelihood |
| Pilot locality service listings | NT Health and SecureNT official published directory pages (plus limited confirmed school listings) | A name/category in a locality is not verified current opening, precise GPS or comprehensive inventory |

## Ethical interpretation

NT Connect is for opening a conversation about priority **investigations**, not labelling a community deficient or automatically choosing investments. It does not collect household-level information, access accounts or identify individuals. Published community-scale information should be interpreted and shared with community consent and consultation with relevant Traditional Owners. A lack of service listing is marked **not established in this limited directory**, not proof that services do not exist. Avoid mapping sensitive community resources without appropriate consent. Infrastructure interventions require community validation, operational feasibility studies and source-date checks.
