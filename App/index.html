Built to work with no signal

Core data and app files stay local after the first visit

The app embeds its core datasets and caches the application shell. Saved location packs are also stored in the browser. This makes the prototype usable when the connection is weak or unavailable.

Online sync→Local cache→Offline use→Refresh later
Using this responsibly
  • The Gap Index is illustrative. It is a transparent triage tool, not a measured signal-quality score or investment verdict.
  • NBN gap means fixed-line gap in this prototype. Fixed wireless and satellite are not represented by the supplied fixed-line layer.
  • Infrastructure presence does not prove reliable service; terrain, backhaul, congestion, devices and weather can all matter.
  • First Nations community mapping should involve consultation on what is shown, to whom, and how, consistent with Indigenous Data Sovereignty principles.
  • This prototype uses community/location-level records and does not model individual or household-level data.
